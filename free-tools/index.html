Free data tools

Small, single-purpose tools for testing APIs, checking statistics and getting a first read on a dataset. Open source, MIT licensed, and most of them run entirely in your browser — no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

No account needed

Everything runs in your browser.

Public API tester

Paste a JSON or XML API URL and see it as a table: the rows, column types, data-quality checks, the response path, and a CSV export.

Public datasets

GDP, inflation, population, life expectancy, weather and solar data from the World Bank, IMF, NOAA, NASA and Open-Meteo — charted, with CSV downloads.

A/B test calculator

Is the variant’s lift real? p-value, confidence interval for the lift, and a sample-ratio-mismatch check.

Statistical significance calculator

Compare two rates or two averages: z-test, Welch t-test and Mann–Whitney, with a plain-language verdict.

Sample size calculator

How many visitors a test needs, and how many days it must run, from your baseline rate and the lift you care about.

Correlation matrix calculator

Pearson or Spearman matrix from a CSV, with p-values, confidence intervals, multiple-testing correction and scatter plots.

What’s in this CSV?

Drop a CSV and get ranked, plain-language findings: trends, correlations, outliers and data-quality flags.
